A trusted manufacturing partner since 1994.

PT Usra Tampi Indonesia is a plastics injection moulding company based in Kawasan Industri Jababeka, Cikarang, West Java. Founded in 1994 with eight machines, we've grown into a facility running 21 injection machines with clamping forces from 100 to 2,400 tons — supported by a fully equipped quality inspection laboratory, from digital calipers to a portable CMM.

Our vision is to compete on quality at both a national and international level, and every process we run is built to support that standard.

  • ISO 9001 & IATF 16949 certified quality system
  • 21 injection machines, 100–2,400 ton capacity
  • Full metrology lab, from caliper to portable CMM
